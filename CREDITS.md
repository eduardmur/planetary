# Credits

## Data

NASA Space Science Data Coordinated Archive (NSSDCA), Planetary Fact Sheets, curated by Dr. David R.
Williams, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center: <https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/>. A work of
the US Government, in the public domain. Planetary Blueprints is unofficial and is not affiliated with
or endorsed by NASA; it uses no NASA insignia.

Positions in the tests are checked against JPL Horizons (<https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/>).

## Lettering

The drawings are lettered with the Hershey Sans 1-stroke font (`src/draw/lettering-data.ts`), from the
SVG conversion by Windell H. Oskay (Evil Mad Scientist) distributed in the
[hersheytext](https://github.com/techninja/hersheytextjs) package (MIT). The font data's own notice:

> The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the U. S. National
> Bureau of Standards. The format of the Font data in this distribution was originally created by
> James Hurt, Cognition, Inc., 900 Technology Park Drive, Billerica, MA 01821
> (mit-eddie!ci-dandelion!hurt).

The glyphs for ♈, ☊, ☉ and ⊥ are drawn in `src/draw/lettering.ts`.
